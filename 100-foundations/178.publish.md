## Pull quote

**Selected line:** "Real users means real data. It also means real obligations."

**Placement:** Immediately after the Concept Panel (P3), before the "You'll See It When" section. No spacer between P3 and P11.

**Format:** Georgia italic 18px, max 52ch, bold 2px top border (text-1 #1C1B18), hairline 1px bottom border (border #D8D5CE). One pull quote per piece.

**Rationale:** This line is the hinge of the piece. Everything before it establishes that a prototype has no real users; everything after explores the consequences of creating real ones. Eye-tracking research (NNGroup) confirms pull quotes capture fixation before body text — placing this here ensures scanner-first readers receive the piece's central claim before the method detail.
