## Pull quote selection

> "The more confidently you refine in a wrong direction, the more polished the wrong thing gets."

Justification: Under 20 words. Self-contained — makes full sense without surrounding context. Names something practitioners experience but rarely say plainly. Captures the practical consequence of the refinement/iteration confusion. Suitable for P11 formatting (bold top border, italic Georgia 18px, hairline bottom, max 52ch).
