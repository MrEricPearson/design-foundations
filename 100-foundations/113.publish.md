## Check-In (P12)

P12 is optional for T100 pieces. Recommended for this piece given its direct try-it-now prompt.

**Question:** Did you write the "I'll know this worked if ___" sentence for something you're currently working on?

**Response options:**
1. Yes — and the blank held something falsifiable
2. Yes — but I had to rewrite it (first attempt was a deliverable or a feeling)
3. Not yet — I'll do it before the next phase

**Placement:** After Try Noticing block, before What Next.
