## Key Distinctions Taught

This piece establishes three critical boundaries:

1. **Observation effect ≠ Observer bias**
   - Observation effect: participant awareness changes behavior (their feeling)
   - Observer bias: facilitator action changes behavior (your doing)
   - Test: "Did you do something, or are they just aware you're there?"

2. **Observation effect ≠ Social desirability bias**
   - Observation effect: what people DO (extra care, persistence, performance)
   - Social desirability bias: what people SAY (claiming they'd use features they avoid)
   - Test: "Are you watching behavior or listening to self-report?"

3. **Performance mode ≠ Natural behavior**
   - Performance: apologizing, explaining, narrating, retrying
   - Natural: closing tab, swearing quietly, moving on
   - Gap reveals observation effect at work
