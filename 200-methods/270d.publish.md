## Check-In question (P12)

**Question:** Did you define the wizard's constraint before running (or planning) the session?

**Three options:**
1. Yes — and mapping what the system will know revealed something I hadn't noticed in the spec
2. Not yet — I have a feature this applies to but haven't tried it
3. Not applicable to my current work

**Placement:** After Proof block (P7), before Take This Further.
**Link note:** Option 1 response may link to the Take This Further channel prompt for sharing findings.
