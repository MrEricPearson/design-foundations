## Synthesis — Revision Priorities

**Worth addressing in revision:**
1. **Name the sources of learning.** The piece currently treats "what v1 revealed" as a given without naming where that revelation typically comes from. A short list — testing, direct feedback, watching someone use it, data — would ground the abstract "learned something specific" language without adding a story. This is a vocabulary/concreteness fix, not a structural one, and doesn't conflict with the no-anecdote rule.
2. **Consider the experiment framing — with a caveat.** "An iteration is an experiment with a result" is a genuinely useful bridge to prior knowledge (the piece already leans on Schön's "carries out an experiment" language at 105-iteration.md:17, so the connective tissue exists). But watch the collision with 200-methods content that treats experiments/hypothesis testing as its own method — check `10-master-outline.md` before making "experiment" load-bearing vocabulary here, so 105 doesn't quietly duplicate a Tier 200 piece's job.

**Flagged but not actioned — single-source, conflicts with a locked project rule:**
3. **"A short concrete example could improve memorability."** This is the same request Alberto made independently on 147 and 177 (see their feedback files) — a real pattern in his reading style, but it runs directly against the project's no-anecdote / pattern-description-over-story rule (`CLAUDE.md`, Recognition Mechanism module in `content-strategy-spec.html`). One reviewer's consistent preference isn't enough evidence to override a deliberate, explicit project rule. Don't add an invented example. If a second, independent reviewer raises the same gap on 105 or another piece, revisit — see `meta/craft-learnings-pending.md`.

**Leave alone:**
- Overall structure, refinement/iteration distinction, and Schön/Nielsen/Jansson-Smith sourcing — no complaints, high clarity score (5/5)
