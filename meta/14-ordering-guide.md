# Ordering Guide — Prerequisite Evaluation and Publishing Sequence

**Version:** 5.0 (updated 2026-08-11 — Second-pass arcs 317-334 added: PM arcs 317-321, Custom Dev arcs 322-325, Non-Custom Dev arcs 326-329, Cross-audience arcs 330-334 including Arc 334 Solution-First Rapid Ideation)
**Prior version:** Pre-audit draft, no longer accurate — do not use for sequencing decisions.

This guide maps prerequisites for every deliverable in the library and produces a recommended publishing sequence. Its only rule: **don't publish a piece before its prerequisite atoms exist.** Everything else is a recommendation.

Consistent with the library's philosophy: this is a recommended path, not a mandated pipeline. Every piece stands alone if read out of order — this guide prevents publishing a piece whose foundational concept hasn't landed yet.

---

## Reading the Table

**Wave:** The earliest wave a piece may publish. A piece at Wave 3 may not publish before all its Wave 1 and Wave 2 prerequisites are available.

**Hard prereqs:** Atoms or pieces that must exist first — without them, a practitioner reading the method would encounter an undefined concept central to the method's execution.

**Soft prereqs:** Atoms that significantly improve the experience of the method but don't make it unusable without them. Publish as close to their associated method as feasible, but the method can go first in a genuine time constraint.
